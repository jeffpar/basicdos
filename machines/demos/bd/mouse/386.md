The machine below is a COMPAQ DeskPro 386 with a 16Mhz 80386, 1Mb of memory (640K of it conventional memory), a VGA Color Display, a 1.2Mb and a 1.44Mb diskette drive, and a 20Mb hard disk, plus a serial mouse on COM2.  It boots the BASIC-DOS Mouse diskette, whose CONFIG.SYS runs PAINT.BAS.  Click the screen to capture the mouse, and hold the left button down to draw; press **Esc** twice to exit.  The boot sector first displays `System starting...`; press any key (or wait 2 seconds) to boot from the diskette, or press **Esc** to boot from the hard disk instead.
