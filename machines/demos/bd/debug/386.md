The machine below is a COMPAQ DeskPro 386 with a 16Mhz 80386, 1Mb of memory (640K of it conventional memory), a VGA Color Display, a 1.2Mb and a 1.44Mb diskette drive, and a 20Mb hard disk.  It boots the BASIC-DOS Serial Debugger diskette with a local console and a debugger on a DEC VT100 connected to COM2 at 9600 baud.  Wait for the debugger's startup message on the VT100, then click its screen and press **Ctrl-C** to stop the machine; type `?` for help, or `G` to resume.  The boot sector first displays `System starting...`; press any key (or wait 2 seconds) to boot from the diskette, or press **Esc** to boot from the hard disk instead.  Like the PC AT, the DeskPro 386 has a battery-powered real-time clock, so BASIC-DOS starts with the current date and time (type `DATE` or `TIME` to see them).
