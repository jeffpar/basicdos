The machine below is a COMPAQ DeskPro 386 with a 16Mhz 80386, 1Mb of memory (640K of it conventional memory), a VGA Color Display, two diskette drives, and a 10Mb hard disk, plus a serial mouse on COM2.  It boots the BASICDOS-360K-S80 diskette and runs PAINT.BAS.  Click the screen to capture the mouse, and hold the left button down to draw; press **Esc** twice to exit.  Since the machine has a hard disk, the boot sector first displays `Press a key to start...`; press any key (or wait 5 seconds) to boot from the diskette, or press **Esc** to boot from the hard disk instead.
