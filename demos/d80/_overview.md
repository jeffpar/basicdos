The BASICDOS-DISK3 diskette's **CONFIG.SYS** defines two 80-column consoles, one above the other (16 rows on top and 9 rows on the bottom), each running its own copy of COMMAND.COM.

Both sessions are configured with borders, and a double-wide border indicates which session has keyboard focus.  Use **SHIFT-TAB** to toggle focus.
